It's 2:14 a.m. The manuscript is due Friday. The threads are breeding.

Tap a card (or press 1–8) to write that thread. You can only write one at a time. Everything else goes cold.

A game about finishing things

Every thread you start is a promise to the reader.

Plot threads get pinned to your board. Tap one to write it. Its draft fills up, and when it hits 100% it's tied off and banked as words.

Every thread you're not writing cools down. Let one go fully cold and it becomes a plot hole. Three plot holes and your editor reads the draft.

Sometimes a shiny new idea floats past. Grabbing it puts you in flow and doubles your writing speed. It also pins a brand-new thread to the board, because of course it does.
